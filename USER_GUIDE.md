## 1. Overview & Philosophy

The **Kids Study Tracker** is designed to transform daily homework and study time from a battle of wills into a positive, habit-forming adventure. 

* **Habit-Building, Not Nagging:** Instead of repeated reminders, children have a clear daily checklist of achievable tasks.
* **Positive Reinforcement:** Completing tasks earns reward stars towards an agreed-upon weekend reward (such as a park visit, special treat, or hobby time).
* **Two-Way Accountability:** Students mark tasks complete and submit them; parents review their work before stars are officially awarded.
* **Curriculum-Aligned:** Preloaded with standard subjects for **Kerala State Syllabus** and **CBSE** from Classes 1 to 12 (Plus Two).

## 3. First-Time Setup & Family Profile

### Registering Your Family Workspace
When creating a new family account on the login page:
1. Click **`Register New Family`**.
2. Enter your **Family Name** (e.g., *Menon Family*), **Parent's Full Name**, **Parent's Email Address**, and choose a secret **4-digit Parent PIN**.
3. A verification email is automatically sent to your registered email address. Open your inbox, click the verification link, and return to the app. The system automatically detects verification and unlocks your workspace immediately!

### Understanding Your Family Code
When your family workspace is set up, a unique **Family Code** (e.g., `KIDS101` or `KIDS485`) is generated. Note that hyphens or spaces are completely optional—kids can type `kids101` or `KIDS-101` and it will automatically work.
* **What is it for?** The Family Code links all your family's devices (mom's phone, dad's phone, child's tablet or phone) to the same private cloud workspace.
* **Where to find it?** The Family Code is always displayed at the top of the Parent Dashboard topbar and in the *Curriculum & Settings* tab.
* **Security:** Keep this code within your family. Anyone with your Family Code and a student/parent PIN can sign in.

## 4. Adding & Managing Student Profiles

Parents can add profiles for multiple children across different classes and syllabi.

### Step-by-Step: Adding a Child
1. Log in to the **Parent Dashboard** using your Parent PIN.
2. In the top bar, click the **`➕ Add Child`** button.
3. Fill in the student details:
   * **Full Name:** Child's name (e.g., Arjun, Ananya).
   * **Avatar:** Choose a cheerful avatar emoji (e.g., 🧒, 👧, 👦, 🎓, 🚀).
   * **Class / Grade:** Select from Standard 1 to 12 (including Plus One & Plus Two).
   * **Curriculum Board:** Select **🌴 Kerala State Syllabus** or **🏛️ CBSE (Central Board)**.
   * **Student PIN:** Choose a 4-digit PIN for the child (e.g., `1234`).
4. Click **`Save Profile`**.

> **Tip:** You can edit a child's grade, PIN, or syllabus at any time by clicking **`✏️ Edit Profile`** on their overview banner.

## 5. Assigning & Managing Daily Study Tasks

The study week runs from **Monday through Friday**, allowing weekends to be focused on celebration and relaxation.

### How to Assign a Task
1. From the Parent Dashboard, click **`➕ Assign Task`** (or go to the *Assign Task* tab).
2. Fill in the assignment details:
   * **Date:** The scheduled date for the task (defaults to today).
   * **Subject:** Preloaded based on the child's grade and syllabus (e.g., Mathematics, Malayalam, English, Basic Science, Social Science).
   * **Category:** Choose the study type (e.g., *Reading & Comprehension*, *Written Homework*, *Revision & Memorization*, *Creative Practice*).
   * **Task Instructions:** Specify what needs to be done (e.g., *"Read Chapter 4 pages 42-48 and complete exercise questions 1 to 4"*).
   * **Reward Points:** Points awarded upon parent approval (customizable, default is usually 10 points).
3. Click **`✅ Assign Task`**.

### Duplicating Routine Tasks
For recurring tasks (e.g., daily math practice or reading), you do not need to retype everything. Simply click the **`📋 Duplicate`** button on any existing task card. It will prefill the *Assign Task* form with all the details ready for scheduling.

### Automatic Task Carryover
If a child cannot finish a task on the assigned day, the system **automatically carries it over** as a `⚠️ Carried Over` or `⚠️ Pending` task on subsequent days. It will remain clearly marked until submitted and approved.

## 6. Student Experience & One-Click Task Submission

When children sign in, they see a colorful, gamified dashboard without adult administrative clutter.

### How a Student Uses the App
1. **Sign In:**
   * Enter the **Family Code** (e.g., `KIDS101`).
   * Enter their personal **4-Digit Student PIN**.
2. **Reviewing Today's Tasks:**
   * Today's assignments are shown with subject icons and point badges.
   * Unfinished tasks from earlier in the week are highlighted at the top.
3. **Submitting Completed Work:**
   * Once a child finishes their homework or reading, they click:
     `📬 Submit for Parent Approval (+10 pts)`.
   * The task status immediately changes to `⏳ Sent to Parent (Pending Approval)`.

## 7. Parent Review, Encouragement Stickers & Approval

To keep standards high, points are not automatically credited when a child clicks submit. The parent must verify the completed study work first.

### Reviewing Submissions
1. When you open the Parent Dashboard, a purple alert banner will inform you:  
   `🔔 [Child Name] submitted X task(s) for your approval!`
2. Under the task card, you will see the **Parent Approval Control Box**:
   * **`✅ Approve (+Points)`**: Click this once you have verified the child's work.
   * **`🔄 Needs Revision`**: If homework is incomplete or needs neatening up, click this. The task will be sent back to the child's pending list with a friendly revision alert.

### Awarding Stickers & Praise Remarks
When approving a task, parents can attach a digital encouragement sticker and a personal note:
* **Stickers:** ⭐ Gold Star, 🌟 Shining Star, 🎯 Target Hit, 🏆 Champ, 🚀 Super Effort, 💯 Perfect.
* **Parent's Remark:** Leave a warm encouraging note (e.g., *"Excellent handwriting today! Proud of you!"*).
* The sticker and remark will proudly display on the child's completed task card!

## 8. Weekend Prizes, Target Stars & Trophy Case

Reward milestones motivate children to sustain their study habits throughout the entire week.

### Setting Up a Weekend Prize
1. Navigate to the **`🏆 Weekend Prize`** tab.
2. Enter the **Prize Name** (e.g., *"Sunday Beach Outing & Ice Cream"*, *"Movie Night with Popcorn"*, or *"New Drawing Book"*).
3. Enter the **Target Points** (e.g., `100` points).
4. Click **`💾 Update Target Goal`**.

### Unlocking & Awarding the Prize
* As tasks are approved Monday through Friday, the child's progress bar fills up.
* When the target is reached, celebratory confetti fires on their screen with the message: `🎉 Target Unlocked!`.
* On the weekend, parents click **`🎁 Mark Reward Given`** to officially record that the prize was enjoyed.
* The prize is archived into the child's permanent **📜 Trophy Case**, preserving a history of all the goals they have achieved!

## 9. Curriculum & Category Customization

The app adapts to whatever textbooks and subjects your child's school uses.

### Managing Subjects
1. Go to the **`⚙️ Curriculum & Categories`** tab.
2. Select the syllabus (**Kerala State** or **CBSE**) and the **Class** (1 to 12 / Plus Two).
3. **Add Subject:** Click `➕ Add Subject` to introduce language subjects (e.g., Sanskrit, Arabic, French, Hindi) or special electives (e.g., Computer Applications, Environmental Studies).
4. **Edit / Delete:** Modify subject names, icons, or remove subjects not taught in your school.
5. **Reset:** If you ever make a mistake, click `🔄 Reset to Defaults` to restore the state-recommended syllabus list.

### Managing Task Categories
Customize the types of study activities and their default point weights:
* *Reading & Understanding* (e.g., 10 pts)
* *Written Homework / Exercises* (e.g., 15 pts)
* *Revision for Exam / Unit Test* (e.g., 20 pts)
* *Creative Project / Drawing* (e.g., 10 pts)

## 10. Data Backup, Restore & Sync

### Real-Time Cloud Sync
Every task created, approved, or edited automatically synchronizes with Google Firebase Firestore. You can view updates on your phone in real time while your child works on their tablet.

### Local JSON Backup & Portability
Parents can download a complete backup file of their family records:
1. Go to **`⚙️ Curriculum & Categories`** ➡️ *Section 3: Local Backup*.
2. Click **`💾 Download Backup (.json)`**.
3. A complete timestamped backup file (containing all student profiles, tasks, curriculum edits, and trophy history) is saved to your computer.
4. If you ever switch browsers or computers, click **`📂 Restore from Backup File`** to restore all records instantly.

## 11. Mobile Installation (PWA on Android & iOS)

You do not need to download the app from Google Play or the Apple App Store. The app uses modern **Progressive Web App (PWA)** technology to install directly from your mobile browser:

### On Android (Google Chrome):
1. Open [https://kids-study-tracker.web.app](https://kids-study-tracker.web.app) in Google Chrome.
2. Tap the three dots menu (**⋮**) in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. The Study Tracker icon will appear on your phone's home screen and open full-screen with no browser address bar!

### On iPhone & iPad (Apple Safari):
1. Open [https://kids-study-tracker.web.app](https://kids-study-tracker.web.app) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.

## 12. Parent Contact Support & Help Desk

If you ever encounter an issue, need help configuring a curriculum, or have a suggestion, you can reach out directly from the application.

### How to Submit a Support Inquiry
1. Log in to the **Parent Dashboard**.
2. Click the **`🎧 Contact Support`** button in the sidebar or top navigation bar.
3. Fill in your inquiry:
   * **Inquiry Category:** Select from *Curriculum & Syllabus Question*, *Task & Daily Routine Help*, *Points, Stars & Rewards*, *Device Login & PIN Assistance*, *Bug Report / Technical Issue*, or *Feature Request / Feedback*.
   * **Subject:** A brief title summarizing your issue.
   * **Message:** Details of your question or problem.
   * **Reply-To Email:** Automatically filled with your verified registered parent email address.
4. Click **`🚀 Submit Support Request`**.

### What Happens Next?
* A unique **Ticket Number** (e.g., `TKT-1042`) is immediately generated and logged under your family's *My Recent Requests* history.
* An instant notification is dispatched to the support team inbox at **`kidsstudytracker@gmail.com`**.
* The administrator reviews your request and replies directly to your registered email address.
* You can monitor the ticket status (`🟢 Open / Needs Reply` or `✅ Resolved`) directly within your *Contact Support* view.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q: How do I contact support or report an issue?
**A:** Navigate to the **`🎧 Contact Support`** tab in the Parent Dashboard. Select a category, enter your message, and click submit. Our support team receives your inquiry at `kidsstudytracker@gmail.com` and replies directly to your registered email address.

#### Q: Why is a parent email address required during registration?
**A:** The parent email address ensures account security, enables verification to prevent spam registrations, and provides a direct channel for the support team to reply to your questions.

#### Q: What if a child forgets their PIN?
**A:** Parents can easily view or reset a child's PIN at any time. In the Parent Dashboard, switch to that child, click **`✏️ Edit Profile`**, enter a new 4-digit PIN, and save.

#### Q: Can both parents manage the tracker from different phones?
**A:** Yes! Both parents can sign in using the same **Family Code** and **Parent PIN** on separate devices. All changes synchronize instantly across both phones.

#### Q: Can our family customize the color theme?
**A:** Absolutely. Click the **`🎨 Theme`** button in the top bar to choose between 6 eye-friendly color palettes: *Classic Indigo*, *Emerald Forest*, *Sunset Amber*, *Ocean Breeze*, *Sweet Candy*, and *Midnight Dark*.

#### Q: Does the app work if the internet temporarily drops?
**A:** Yes. The application caches essential data locally on the device (via Service Worker and LocalStorage). When your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or mobile data, updates automatically sync to the cloud.
